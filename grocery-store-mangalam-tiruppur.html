Trusted Grocery Store in Mangalam Since Day One

Central & Stores is a family-run grocery and department store serving Mangalam and the wider Tiruppur area. We stock rice, flours, pulses, oils, masalas, snacks, beverages, personal care, baby care, home care and daily essentials under one roof. Our goal is simple — give the neighbourhood a store they can rely on for quality products at honest prices, with same-day home delivery when it matters.

Walk in to our shop at 1/90 C Tirupur Main Road, Mangalam, or order by phone or WhatsApp and we will deliver to your doorstep the same day. Every order is personally verified by the shop owner before delivery, so you get exactly what you asked for at the confirmed price.

Delivery Across Mangalam and Tiruppur

We deliver free of charge to every street in Mangalam. No petrol, no service charge, no hidden fee. Just add your items to the order, place a minimum ₹1000 order, and our team brings it to your door. If you live outside Mangalam but within Tiruppur, we still deliver — a small petrol allowance applies on top of the order.

Same-day delivery is our standard. Orders placed in the morning reach you the same afternoon. Orders placed during the day reach you by evening. If you have an urgent need, say so when ordering and we will prioritise your delivery — often within 30 minutes.
